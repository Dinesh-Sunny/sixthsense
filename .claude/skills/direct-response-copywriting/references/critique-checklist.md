# Critique Checklist

Run on every draft (yours or the user's). Report each issue as: problem → why it hurts → concrete rewrite.

1. Right audience? Copy matched to awareness level (enthusiast list = jump in; cold broad audience = ease in)?
2. Headline promises a specific, desirable result or opens a curiosity loop — about *them*?
3. Opening talks about the reader before the writer?
4. Every feature translated to benefit → deeper benefit, concretely?
5. Enough bullets, including blind/curiosity bullets?
6. Story with specifics and a discovery moment?
7. Greased slide: any paragraph where a reader could comfortably stop?
8. Can a skimmer get the pitch from headline + subheads alone?
9. Offer clear, risk removed, reason-why given, one easy call to action?
10. Key points repeated ~3×? Specific numbers instead of round ones?
11. Cute lines, adjective piles, or clever writing to cut?
12. Every key line: can you visualize it, falsify it, and could nobody else say it?
13. Gets it in 2 seconds, checked in its real context (feed, inbox, among other ads)?
14. Any sentence removable without loss? Any "and" diluting the idea?
15. Desire evidenced outside our marketing? Every claim a real proposition competitors can't copy, or deleted?
16. Problem names an accommodation (escalating, one person)? Promise precedes mechanism? Proof ≥ claim?
17. One awareness/sophistication level, consistent across visual, headline, body, landing page?
18. "So what?" ends at a human reason/identity? Read aloud without tripping?
19. Obvious next test to recommend?

## Ethics guardrails

No fabricated testimonials, credentials (e.g. posing as a doctor), results, scarcity, "damaged stock," or fake news articles implying independent endorsement. Reason-why and editorial formats must be true; label advertorials where law or platform requires. Flag compliance risk (FTC, health, financial claims) instead of writing it silently.
