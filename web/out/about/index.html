SixthSense

Your cameras record everything. SixthSense tells you what happens next, then checks if it was right.

See it on real footageHow it works
Step 1

It watches every clip

Highways, crossings, quiet streets. It searches the indexed footage for risky moments across every camera, so nothing waits for a person to press play.

Under the hood: semantic search over the VAST video archive.

Step 2

It rules out false alarms

Before anyone is bothered, it tracks every car and person frame by frame. If two of them aren't really closing in, the moment is dropped.

Under the hood: YOLO11 boxes and a tracker measure the gap in car lengths.

Step 3

It sees what happens next

For every real moment, it predicts the next few seconds, says why, and suggests one practical fix. In plain words, with the vehicle and the time.

Under the hood: Cosmos3-Reason watches the clip and answers in a fixed format.

Step 4

Then it checks itself

It watches the next clip and marks each prediction: came true, partly, or didn't happen. You get a score measured on your own footage, not a promise.

Under the hood: the next clip is judged against the prediction, and the score is saved.

Score

Each prediction gets checked against the clip that comes next. Scores appear as clips arrive.

Built on

VAST VastDB

Keeps every moment, prediction and score, so the dashboard and the record are the same thing.

NVIDIA Cosmos3-Reason

Watches each clip, says what happens next and why, then watches the next clip to check.

YOLO11 on CoreWeave

Finds the cars and people in every frame, so close calls are measured, not guessed.

W&B Weave

Records every model call and every score, so each prediction can be traced back.
